HOW TO PLAY
Objective

Slide tiles to restore the image. The empty cell is the destination — only tiles adjacent to it can move. Tiles must end in order (left→right, top→bottom).

Mouse / Touch
🖱️Click / tap any tile next to the empty space to slide it.
Hand Tracking Gestures
☝Point: Extend index finger. The cyan cursor follows your fingertip.
🤌Pinch: Bring thumb + index finger together to slide the hovered tile.
⏳Dwell: Hold finger still on a valid tile for ~0.8 s — auto-slides without pinching.
✋Open palm: Pauses gesture input. Useful to reposition your hand.
Tips
💡Good lighting on your hand improves detection accuracy.
